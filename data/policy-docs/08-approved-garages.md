# Approved garages and repair estimates

An approved garage is a repair shop that works with Contoso. Using one is optional, but it makes the claim faster.

## With an approved garage

- The garage sends the estimate to us directly.
- We pay the garage; you pay only your deductible.
- Repairs carry a 3-year workmanship guarantee from Contoso.

## With your own garage

- Send us a written, itemised repair estimate before any work starts.
- We may ask for a second estimate if the first one is above $5,000.
- We pay you after the repair, against the final invoice.

You can find approved garages near you in the customer portal. Do not start repairs before the damage assessment is finished, except for small repairs needed to drive the car safely (up to $300).
