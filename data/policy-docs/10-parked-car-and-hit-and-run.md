# Damage while parked, and hit-and-run

A **parked car** claim is for damage that happens while the car is parked and nobody is in it: another car hits it, a falling branch, vandalism.

- Look for witnesses and for a note left by the other driver.
- Take photos of the damage and the location.
- If you think a crime was committed (vandalism, hit-and-run), report it to the police and send us the police report number.

## Hit-and-run while driving

If another driver hits you and drives away, report it to the police within 24 hours. The claim is handled as a collision claim, and you pay your deductible unless the other driver is later found.

Parked-car claims don't affect your no-claims discount if the other driver is identified.
