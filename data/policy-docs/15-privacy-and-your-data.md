# Privacy and your data

## What the claims assistant can see

After you sign in, the assistant can see the claims linked to your account: the status, the step, and which documents are still missing. It cannot see anyone else's claims, and it will never share another person's claim information, even if you give it their claim number.

## What we keep

- Chat conversations are kept for 90 days, then deleted.
- Documents and photos you upload are kept with the claim for 7 years after it closes, as the law requires.
- Summaries of your conversation, with personal details masked, are shown to claims handlers to help them prepare a callback.

## Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, or ask us to correct it, through the customer portal. Data we must keep by law can't be deleted before the end of the retention period.
