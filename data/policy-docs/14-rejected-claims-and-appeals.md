# Rejected claims and appeals

We reject a claim when the incident isn't covered by the policy. The most common reasons:

- The driver wasn't named on the policy or didn't have a valid licence.
- The car was used for paid deliveries or ride-sharing without business cover.
- The damage happened before the policy started.
- Information given on the claim was false. Suspected fraud is passed to our investigations team.
- Theft with the keys left in or on the car.

## How to appeal

1. Reply to the rejection letter within **60 days**, saying why you think the decision is wrong, with any new evidence.
2. A senior handler who didn't work on the claim reviews it within 15 working days.
3. If you still disagree, you can complain to your state's Department of Insurance.

The claims assistant can't overturn a decision, but it can book a callback with a handler to discuss it.
