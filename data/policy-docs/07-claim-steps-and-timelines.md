# Claim steps and how long they take

The claims assistant shows two things for each claim: a **status** and the **step** the claim is at.

| Status | Step | What it means | Usual time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Awaiting documents | Documents | We are waiting for one or more documents from you. | Until all documents arrive |
| Open | Damage assessment | An assessor is reviewing photos, the repair estimate, and possibly the car. | 5–10 working days |
| Open | Settlement | We are agreeing the amount covered, minus your deductible. | 3–5 working days |
| Closed | Paid | The claim is paid to you or to the garage. | — |
| Closed | Rejected | The claim is not covered. You get a letter with the reasons. | — |

Bank transfers arrive within 3 working days of the claim moving to *Paid*.

If your claim has been at the same step for longer than the usual time, ask the claims assistant for a callback.
