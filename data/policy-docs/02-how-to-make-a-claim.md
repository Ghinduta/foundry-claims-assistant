# How to make a claim

1. **Make sure everyone is safe.** Call 911 if anyone is hurt. Call the police if another driver leaves the scene, if you suspect a crime, or if the vehicle was stolen.
2. **Report the claim within 30 days** of the incident, through the customer portal or the claims line. Late reports can still be accepted, but we may ask why it was late.
3. **Send the required documents.** What you need depends on what happened; see *Documents you need for a claim*. Upload them in the portal.
4. **Damage assessment.** An assessor reviews your photos and repair estimate, and may inspect the car.
5. **Settlement.** We confirm what is covered, subtract your deductible, and agree the amount with you or with the garage.
6. **Payment.** We pay the garage directly if you use an approved garage, or pay you by bank transfer.

You will get a claim number in the format `CLM-` followed by four digits, for example CLM-1234. Quote it whenever you contact us.

Do not admit fault at the scene, and do not agree to pay the other driver directly.
