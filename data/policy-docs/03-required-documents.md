# Documents you need for a claim

Every claim needs a **signed claim form**. The other documents depend on the type of incident.

| Incident type | Documents |
|---|---|
| Single vehicle collision | Signed claim form, photos of the damage, repair estimate from an approved garage |
| Multi-vehicle collision | Signed claim form, photos of the damage, repair estimate from an approved garage, the other driver's name, plate number and insurer |
| Parked car (damaged while parked, driver not present) | Signed claim form, photos of the damage, repair estimate from an approved garage |
| Vehicle theft | Signed claim form, police report number, vehicle registration certificate, all keys to the vehicle |

**If the police attended or were called**, for any incident type, also send the police report number.

## Photos of the damage

Take at least four photos: the whole car from the front and from the back, and close-ups of each damaged area. Make sure the plate number is visible in at least one photo. Photos must be JPEG or PNG and under 20 MB each.

## Missing documents

Your claim stays at the *documents* step until everything is in. The claims assistant can tell you which documents are still missing on your claim.
