# Umbrella cover

Umbrella cover is an optional extra that pays liability claims above your liability limit. It is sold in steps of $1 million, from $3 million to $9 million.

- It pays only after your liability limit has been used up in full.
- It does not cover damage to your own car.
- It has no separate deductible.

Most customers don't have umbrella cover. If it is on your policy, the umbrella limit is shown on your policy schedule; if the schedule shows 0, you don't have it.
