# Vehicle theft

If your car is stolen:

1. Report the theft to the police straight away and get a **police report number**.
2. Report the claim to us within 48 hours of finding the car missing.
3. Send the signed claim form, the police report number, the vehicle registration certificate and **all keys** to the vehicle.

## If the car is not found

We wait 30 days from the report. If the car has not been found by then, we treat it as a total loss and pay its market value at the time of the theft, minus your deductible. See *Total loss*.

## If the car is found

If it is found before we pay, the claim becomes a damage claim for any damage caused during the theft. If it is found after we pay, the car belongs to Contoso.

## Not covered

Theft when the keys were left in or on the car, or the car was left unlocked.
