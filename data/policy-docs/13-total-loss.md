# Total loss

A car is a **total loss** when repairing it would cost more than 75% of its market value, or when it is stolen and not found within 30 days.

## What we pay

The market value of the car just before the incident, minus your deductible. Market value is based on the make, model, year and mileage, from an independent valuation guide.

## What happens next

1. The assessor confirms the total loss.
2. We send you a settlement offer within 10 working days.
3. If you accept, you sign the car over to Contoso and send all keys and the registration certificate.
4. We pay within 3 working days of receiving them. If there is a loan on the car, we pay the lender first.

If you don't agree with the valuation, you can send us two independent valuations within 14 days of the offer.
