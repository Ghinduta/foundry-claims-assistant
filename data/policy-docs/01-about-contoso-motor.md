# About Contoso Motor Insurance

Contoso Motor Insurance is a fictional car insurer, used here for a demo. It sells private car policies in Ohio, Illinois and Indiana.

## Contact

- Claims line: 0800 555 0142, Monday to Friday 08:00–20:00, Saturday 09:00–13:00 (Eastern Time).
- Emergency roadside help: 0800 555 0199, 24 hours a day, every day.
- Online: sign in to the customer portal to chat with the claims assistant, upload documents and see the status of your claims.

## Asking for a callback

If you would rather talk to a person, ask the claims assistant for a callback and give a reason and a preferred time. A claims handler reviews every callback request before it is booked. You get a confirmation once a handler has approved it, usually within one working day.

## What the claims assistant can and cannot do

The assistant answers questions about our policies and processes, and tells you the status of your own claims after you sign in. It cannot change a claim, approve a payment, or share any information about another person's claim.
