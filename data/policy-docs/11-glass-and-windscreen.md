# Glass and windscreen damage

Windscreen and window damage is covered by every Contoso policy.

| Repair | Deductible | Effect on no-claims discount |
|---|---|---|
| Chip repair (chip smaller than a quarter, not in the driver's line of sight) | None | None |
| Windscreen replacement | $100 | None |
| Side or rear window replacement | $100 | None |

You don't need a claim form for glass-only damage; book the repair through the customer portal. Sunroofs and panoramic roofs are covered as body damage, with your normal deductible.
