# Rental car while yours is repaired

If your car can't be driven after a covered incident, we pay for a rental car:

- Up to **$40 per day**, for at most **30 days**.
- Only while the car is being repaired at a garage, or for a theft, until the claim is settled.
- The rental car must be booked through Contoso's rental partner in the customer portal.

Fuel, tolls and extra insurance on the rental car are not covered. If your car is a total loss, the rental stops 5 days after we make the settlement offer.
