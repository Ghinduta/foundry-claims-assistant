# Liability limits

Liability cover pays for injuries to other people that you cause while driving. Your limit is written as two numbers, in thousands of dollars: the most we pay **per person** and the most we pay **per accident**.

| Limit on your schedule | Per injured person | Per accident |
|---|---|---|
| 100/300 | $100,000 | $300,000 |
| 250/500 | $250,000 | $500,000 |
| 500/1000 | $500,000 | $1,000,000 |

Damage to your own car is not paid from these limits; it is covered by your collision cover, minus your deductible.

If a claim against you is bigger than your limit, an umbrella policy can pay the rest; see *Umbrella cover*.
