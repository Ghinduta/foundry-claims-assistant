# Your deductible

The deductible (also called the excess) is the part of each claim you pay yourself. We subtract it from the amount we pay.

Contoso policies come with a deductible of **$500, $1,000 or $2,000**, chosen when you buy the policy. A higher deductible means a lower annual premium. Your deductible is shown on your policy schedule.

## When you pay no deductible

- Windscreen chip repair (not replacement); see *Glass and windscreen damage*.
- A multi-vehicle collision where the other driver is found fully at fault and their insurer pays us back. We refund your deductible once we recover it.

## Example

Repairs cost $3,400 and your deductible is $1,000. We pay $2,400; you pay $1,000 to the garage.

You can change your deductible at renewal, not in the middle of a policy year.
